# ADHD presentation

One audience. One ask. Five slides maximum.

## State line, print first

NOW:
DONE:
NEXT:
BLOCKED:

## Slides

1. Title and the ask, one sentence.
2. What is true, with the source named.
3. What changed, one comparison.
4. What is unknown. Do not fill the gap.
5. The decision requested, owner, and date if given. Else UNKNOWN.

## Steps

1. Name audience and the decision they must make. If unnamed, ask one question and stop.
2. Use only points marked FACT or CHECKED. INFERENCE stays off the slide, in a speaker note.
3. One idea per slide. Max 12 words in the title. Max 4 lines in the body.
4. Speaker note is the sentence to say, not a second essay.
5. Print the state line. NEXT is rehearse slide 1, or STOP.

## Do not

- Do not add a slide to look complete.
- Do not polish an unverified number into a claim.
