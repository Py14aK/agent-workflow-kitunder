# Research rederive

Use for mathematical physics, theoretical physics, scientific claims, equations, paper audits, reproducibility, and consistency checks.

## Workflow

1. Identify the exact claim and authoritative source.
2. Record notation, assumptions, conventions, and dependencies.
3. Decompose the claim into atomic statements.
4. Re-derive independently rather than paraphrasing the source.
5. Check dimensions, limiting cases, signs, conventions, and boundary conditions.
6. Reproduce numerically or symbolically when useful.
7. Perform at least one adversarial/consistency test.
8. Classify each key result with the epistemic legend.
9. Finish with the single most decisive unresolved check.

## Output

- claim ledger
- assumptions
- derivation
- reproduction status
- adversarial check
- discrepancies
- strongest next test

Never label generated reasoning as executed or source-verified unless it was actually checked.
