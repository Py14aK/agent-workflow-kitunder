# Research publisher

Use after verification. If central claims are not validated, route to `research-rederive` first.

## Workflow

1. Audit the source dossier for facts, derivations, inference, uncertainty, and contradictions.
2. Choose narrative order for the intended audience.
3. Preserve citations next to supported factual and quantitative claims.
4. Keep necessary equations in the main narrative and move long derivations to appendices when useful.
5. Create charts only when they materially improve comprehension and retain source, units, transformations, and reproducibility path.
6. Inspect the final artifact when rendering tools are available.
7. Verify numbers, equations, citations, links, captions, and uncertainty markers against the validated source dossier.

Do not polish uncertainty away or introduce unsupported claims for narrative flow.
